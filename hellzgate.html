The Platform

Observe. Locate. Log locally.

FullGate brings multiple radios together for wireless research, education, and authorized testing. Its firmware foundation observes Wi-Fi and Bluetooth LE; complete standalone operation is still being verified.

Wireless observation

Passive Wi-Fi observation on 2.4 and 5 GHz, plus Bluetooth LE. One master coordinates nine removable scanner modules.

Local records

Four nine-scanner field runs saved 423,592 observations across I²C and ESP-NOW. CSV structure and timestamps checked; startup rows without valid coordinates remain identified.

Connected locally

I²C is the primary communication backbone within FullGate. ESP-NOW provides a secondary wireless path. Both transports completed nine-scanner bench and field logging. A historical 20-scanner bench result is documented below; current-firmware scale testing remains pending.

Hardware · Planned Release

The FullGate Core Board.

The first planned HellzGate product is a core board, not a complete ready-to-use kit. No orders or release date announced.

Board design

  • Ten removable XIAO ESP32-C5 module positions: one master and nine scanners
  • Onboard GNSS support and microSD logging
  • USB-C PD input and externally charged 4S lithium-ion battery input through XT60
  • 3.3 V I²C display connector and cooling-fan support

Extended subsystem checks and measured battery runtime remain in progress.

Separate accessories

XIAO modules, antennas, display, fan, power supply, battery, and enclosure are not included with the planned Core Board.

No preorders or waitlists. Availability will be announced on GitHub and Discord after testing.

HellzGate ESP-NOW Cluster Firmware — Open-Source Beta

Master + scanner/node firmware for ESP32-C5.

Our ESP-NOW Cluster Firmware beta is now open source under MIT. Explore the code, fork it, adapt it to your hardware, and help move it forward. From the HellzGate Project by Hellz (Sean Clossey).

Fork it. Build it. Share what you learn.

Source, build instructions and tests for an ESP32-C5 master and scanner nodes collecting passive Wi-Fi and Bluetooth LE observations. XIAO ESP32-C5 boards and custom builds are welcome; the FullGate PCB is not required for the ESP-NOW transport. Check the hardware adaptation guide before using other pin layouts.

Experimental. Not a final release.

The supplied builds are configured for up to 20 scanners. Earlier firmware demonstrated 20-scanner operation; this beta still needs its own 20-scanner field validation. Limited master/one-scanner bench testing passed before the final changes, which were rebuilt but not reflashed.

This is a source-only ESP-NOW beta, separate from the production firmware and app integration. Read the known limitations and share test results or issues on GitHub.
